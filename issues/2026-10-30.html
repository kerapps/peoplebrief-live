The latest data paints a worrying picture for U.S. employee engagement—it's at a 15-year low. Leaders need to understand the causes and start addressing them. Today’s brief dives into the numbers and offers practical steps to keep your teams involved.

Post · Fast Company Work Life

Employee engagement in U.S. hits lowest point in 15 years↗

U.S. worker engagement has dropped to its lowest since 2011, according to Gallup. Low engagement can lead to turnover and productivity loss, so it's critical to figure out what's driving the dip. Keep in mind these figures might not reflect all sectors equally; the study highlights remote and hybrid workers in AI-driven organizations as being hit hardest.

✎ HR Tip of the Day

When running your next engagement survey, ask a fresh question: 'What's one thing we could do better to support you as a remote/hybrid employee right now?' Focus directly on the needs of remote and hybrid workers, as they’ve shown the sharpest dip in engagement.
