✎ HR Tip of the Day

This week, ask each team member to dedicate 10 minutes to a new skill they think AI might impact. Use this as a test run for Dropbox-style cognitive training initiatives. It’s a small, low-risk step to keeping minds active and open in a tech-driven workplace.
